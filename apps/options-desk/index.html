README: refreshing and what is not covered

Refresh

  • Prices, VWAP and RSI update on their own about every 30 seconds while the page is visible. Contract quotes, account value and positions update on the same beat.
  • Refresh re-pulls every panel at once. Each panel shows the time of its last successful pull.
  • The liquidity rank and top pick refresh every two minutes and on Refresh. The page opens on the top pick until you tap a ticker yourself.
  • Dealer levels are computed once per trading day and kept in this browser. Recompute levels rebuilds them from the chain as it stands now; the first build of the day takes up to a minute because SPY and QQQ carry several hundred contracts.
  • Earnings dates and the economic calendar load once per page open.
  • Choose your account once in Open positions. The page remembers it in this browser; no account number is stored in the page itself.

Not covered

  • Orders. The page can only read; it holds no tool that previews, places, cancels or exercises anything.
  • Spreads, short options and assigned stock. Sizing and stops assume one long call or one long put.
  • Market holidays and half days. The 3:45 PM ET countdown runs on the clock alone.
  • Intraday rebuilds of the dealer map, air pockets, vanna and charm tables, and the heatseeker entry gates. Open interest is published a day late, so levels describe yesterday's board adjusted only by the stability check.
  • Dividends, early exercise and fees in break-even. Break-even is strike plus or minus the ask.
  • Direction. The top pick is the most liquid ticker, not a call-or-put signal.
  • Slippage. Stops and scale-outs are premium levels, not resting orders.
  • Tickers other than SPY, QQQ, NVDA, TSLA, AMD and META.
